# GitLama brand

`icon.svg` is the full-bleed icon master, copied by the generator from the vendored `resources/brand/app-icon-gitlama.svg`. `feature-graphic.svg` is the editable 1024×500 feature master used for website previews and `social-card.svg` is the editable 1200×630 social master. The masters share the cerulean ground (`#056CB3`, Meridian's light accent) and the ivory Lama standing on a Git merge; the social card sets the icon tile on the slate brand ground (`#14242B`), read from the design tokens.

Run `scripts/generate-brand.py` with librsvg and ImageMagick installed. It generates PNGs, the macOS asset catalog images, the Windows ICO, website copies and a SHA-256 manifest. `icon-*.png` are full-bleed, for the website. `desktop-icon-*.png` come from the vendored rounded desktop master with a transparent margin; they feed the macOS catalog, the Windows ICO and the Linux packages and portable tar, because those platforms apply no mask. `tile-*.png` are the rounded tile without a margin, for the app icon shown inside the app. Keep masters and derivatives together. The verifier checks dimensions, alpha policy, platform copies and source drift without requiring raster tools in CI.

The P10-W01 review accepted the mark at 16, 32, 64, 256 and 1024 pixels: the north arrow and N silhouette remain distinct at the smallest size, while the white start node becomes a single readable accent rather than fine detail. Icons intentionally use transparent corners around the dark rounded square. Feature and social graphics are fully opaque and keep text clear of the mark. Platform-specific masking is left to each desktop shell; the master does not embed a second platform shape.
