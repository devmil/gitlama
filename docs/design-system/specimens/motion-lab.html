Meridian Motion Lab

Every moment in the Meridian motion guide (MM-xx) and GitLama's motion spec (NH-x, NM-xx), running with the real tokens. Use slow motion to read the timing and switch on Reduce motion to check each fallback. Everything is interruptible: click again mid-animation.

Foundations

Four curves, three springs and one duration scale. Springs have no fixed duration; the plots mark where each one is within 1% of rest.

curves

Timing curves

Balls travel for 600 ms so the curves are comparable. In the UI the curves run at their token durations.

springs

Springs (mass 1)

Flutter: SpringDescription.withDampingRatio(mass: 1, stiffness, ratio). Web: a generated linear() easing sampled every 8 ms.

durations

Duration scale

Click a row to play that duration. Everything in the working UI stays under 320 ms; motion-celebrate and motion-attention are the only exceptions.

Controls

Built into the meridian_ui controls, so every app gets them.

Hover and press

Hover in motion-hover 80 ms, out motion-hover-out 160 ms. Press to 0.97 in 60 ms (exit), release on spring-snappy. Keyboard Space plays a press.

MM-03 · 04

Loading and success

Takes 1.4 s

The spinner waits motion-spinner-delay 150 ms. Width follows over motion-layout. Success draws a check in 160 ms, holds 900 ms, then crossfades back.

MM-06 · 07

Toolbar nudge and badge

Nudge: 2 px in the icon's direction over 90 ms, spring back (Refresh turns 180°). Badge appears on spring-pop, rolls on change, shrinks out on motion-exit.

Text field

Focus halo grows 0 → 1 px in 100 ms. Submitting empty shakes 3 px × 3 in 240 ms and reveals the message. Errors while typing (a space) never shake. The count rolls.

Lists and navigation

Indicators travel between items instead of jumping.

MM-20 · 21 · 22 · 23

List

Arrow keys or click: the bar glides on spring-snappy; Home and End jump more than 8 rows and crossfade. Hover actions slide 4 px in. Inserts and removals reveal over motion-layout. An outside change tints once over 900 ms.

MM-24 · 26 · 27

Disclosure and tabs

Tab pill glides. A new tab's width grows from 0 over motion-layout; a closed tab fades, then collapses. Expanding a section rotates the chevron and staggers the first 6 rows by 18 ms.

Overlays

Overlays grow out of their anchor and leave faster than they came.

MM-16 · 40 · 41 · 42

Menu

Opens over motion-overlay 140 ms on the enter curve: fade, scale 0.98 → 1 and 4 px from the anchor edge. Closes in 100 ms. The highlight glides between rows; arrow keys work.

MM-45 · 46 · 47

Dialog

Scrim and surface over motion-dialog 200 ms; the surface scales 0.96 → 1 and rises 8 px. Press Enter while Delete is disabled: the surface shakes 4 px × 3 in 280 ms.

MM-49 · 50

Toasts

Arrive from 12 px on spring-gentle; the stack shifts on the same spring. The time rule on action toasts pauses while hovered. Leaving moves 8 px outwards in 100 ms.

Feedback and containers

Fast work never flashes a spinner; slow work shows progress that settles instead of snapping.
